# Privacy Policy for PlateQuest

**Last updated:** September 6, 2026

PlateQuest (“the App”) is developed by Gregory Steven Mendez.

## Summary
PlateQuest is a personal road-trip license plate game. Trip data stays on your device. We do not run a PlateQuest account system or sell your data.

## Information the App may access
- **Location (when in use):** If you allow it, the App may save the approximate location where you logged a license plate during a trip. Location is stored on your device with that trip.
- **Photos (optional):** If you choose to attach a photo to a plate find, that photo is stored on your device with the trip.
- **Player names and trip details:** Names and trip notes you enter are stored on your device.

## What we do not do
- We do not require you to create an online account to use the App.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the App.

## Data storage and sharing
Trip data, photos, and location tags are stored locally on your device (and in your private iCloud backups if you use device backup). They are not uploaded to a PlateQuest server by the App.

If you contact support by email, we will use your contact details only to respond.

## Children’s privacy
The App may be used by families. Do not enter information about children that you do not want stored on the shared device. We do not knowingly collect information from children online through a PlateQuest backend, because there is no such backend.

## Your choices
You can deny location and photo permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete trips (and associated data) inside the App, or delete the App to remove local data.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

## Contact
Questions about privacy: use the Support URL listed on the App Store product page, or contact Gregory Steven Mendez via the developer contact information in App Store Connect.
